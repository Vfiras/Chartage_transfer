DESTINATIONS MENTIONED FOR ACCESSIBLE (WHEELCHAIR) EXCURSIONS

The accessible-tourism service specifically organizes excursions to: Carthage (archaeological
site), Sidi Bou Saïd, Tunis Medina, La Marsa, Bardo Museum, Hammamet, Nabeul, and Sousse Medina,
among other attractions. Drivers for this service are trained on accessible routes, ramps, and
wheelchair-friendly entrances at these specific locations.
