CARTHAGE TRANSFER — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
[Source: carthage-transfer.com — compiled from live site FAQ sections, June 2026]

---

GENERAL / AIRPORT TRANSFERS

Q: Where can I find my driver at the Tunis-Carthage International Airport?
A: Upon exiting the baggage claim area, head towards the main terminal exit. Your driver will be
waiting outside, holding a sign with your name or the Carthage Transfer logo for easy identification.

Q: What if my flight is delayed or arrives early?
A: We continuously monitor flight schedules using the flight number you provide. If your flight is
delayed or arrives early, we adjust your pickup time accordingly, ensuring your driver is ready
when you are.

Q: How much time do I have to meet my driver?
A: You have 1 hour of free waiting time after your flight lands. Waiting time only begins once the
flight has actually landed — delays before landing are never charged. Each additional 30 minutes of
waiting beyond the free hour is charged at 5 EUR (i.e. approximately 10 EUR per additional hour).

Q: Can I change or cancel my reservation?
A: Yes. You can modify or cancel your booking up to 24 hours before your scheduled transfer free of
charge. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before pickup may be subject to charges. For any
booking change, contact Carthage Transfer via WhatsApp.

Q: What type of vehicles do you offer for airport transfers?
A: Our fleet ranges from compact sedans to large coaches. Available categories include Standard
Sedan, Renault Express, Toyota Yaris, VW Multivan, Mercedes E-Class, Mercedes S-Class, Mercedes
V-Class, Toyota Prado, Toyota Hiace, Toyota Coaster, Otokar, and the MAN A51 coach. Specify your
preferred category when booking and we will accommodate group size and luggage needs.

Q: Is the tip included in the transfer price?
A: No. Tipping is not included in the fare. If you are satisfied with the service, tipping the
driver is customary but entirely at your discretion.

Q: Do you offer transfers to other locations besides the airport?
A: Yes. Beyond airport transfers, we offer nationwide intercity transportation, experiential tours,
corporate travel, special-event transport, chauffeur services, and bus rentals across Tunisia.

Q: What safety measures are in place due to health concerns?
A: Our vehicles are cleaned and sanitized after each ride. Drivers follow health protocols including
wearing masks and using hand sanitizer regularly when required.

EXPERIENTIAL TOURS

Q: What are Experiential Tours with Carthage Transfer?
A: Experiential Tours are personalized, private, guided travel experiences that go beyond standard
sightseeing — immersing you in Tunisia's culture, history, cuisine, and natural beauty in comfort
and style, with a private vehicle and an expert local guide.

Q: How do I book an experiential tour?
A: You can book directly through the website, via WhatsApp, or by contacting customer service.
Provide your interests and preferred travel dates and the team will arrange the rest.

NATIONWIDE / INTERCITY TRANSFERS

Q: What types of vehicles do you offer for long-distance transfers?
A: The same fleet used for airport transfers is available for intercity travel — sedans, vans, and
larger vehicles for groups or families. Specify your preference when booking.

Q: What are the main routes available?
A: We cover all major Tunisian cities and tourist destinations: Tunis, Sousse, Hammamet, Sfax,
Djerba, Tozeur, and more. Customized routes can be arranged on request.

Q: How early should I book my intercity transfer?
A: Last-minute bookings are accepted when possible, but we recommend reserving at least 24 hours
ahead to guarantee vehicle availability and smooth planning.

SPECIAL EVENTS

Q: What types of special events do you provide transportation for?
A: Weddings, corporate events, private parties, VIP receptions, group airport transfers, and more.
Service is tailored to the specific event.

Q: How far in advance should I book for a special event?
A: As early as possible — ideally several weeks ahead, especially during peak season — to ensure
vehicle and driver availability.

Q: Can you accommodate large groups for events?
A: Yes. Our fleet includes vans and minibuses (up to the Toyota Hiace, Toyota Coaster, Otokar, and
MAN A51 coach) for large groups travelling together.

Q: How do I pay for the service?
A: Securely online via the website, by bank transfer, or in person, depending on preference. Details
are provided at booking.

Q: Can I request specific vehicles for my event?
A: Yes — you can choose from the full fleet, including luxury sedans, SUVs, and vans, based on group
size and preference.

Q: Can I modify the pickup time or location on the day of the event?
A: Contact Carthage Transfer as early as possible with any change; the team will do their best to
accommodate updates.

Q: Can we book a chauffeur for a full day?

Q: Can services be customized to a company's specific requirements?

Q: Do you provide VIP chauffeur services for special events?

WHEELCHAIR ACCESSIBLE TRANSPORT

Q: Are your vehicles fully adapted for wheelchair users?
A: Yes. Accessible vehicles are equipped with ramps or electric lifts, certified wheelchair
anchoring systems, wide doors, and spacious interiors, accommodating manual and electric wheelchairs
as well as mobility frames.

Q: Will the driver assist me with getting in and out of the vehicle?
A: Yes. Drivers are trained to assist passengers with reduced mobility from the door to the vehicle
and back, including boarding, seat positioning, securing the wheelchair, and handling mobility aids.

Q: Can I book an accessible transfer for someone else?
A: Yes. Families, caregivers, hotels, and medical facilities frequently book on behalf of passengers
with mobility limitations; special requirements are followed for a safe, comfortable journey.
