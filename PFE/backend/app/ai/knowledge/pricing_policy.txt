CURRENCY

All prices on the public Carthage Transfer website are quoted in EUR (euros), not TND. Confirm
with the client whether they expect EUR or local-currency pricing if there is any ambiguity.

WAITING TIME POLICY

- 1 hour of free waiting time is included after the flight has landed.
- Waiting time only starts counting after landing — delays before landing are never charged.
- Beyond the free hour, additional waiting is charged at 5 EUR per 30 minutes (approx. 10 EUR/hour).
- No charge applies for the flight delay itself, only for waiting beyond the free allowance after
  landing.

---

CANCELLATION & MODIFICATION

- Free cancellation with full refund if cancelled at least 24 hours before scheduled pickup.
- Cancellations made less than 24 hours before pickup may incur charges (the published Terms &
  Conditions do not specify an exact percentage — described only as "may be subject to charges").
- Booking changes should be made via WhatsApp contact with the team.

---

PAYMENT

- Payments can be made securely online, by bank transfer, or in person, depending on client
  preference. No tipping is included in any quoted fare.

COMPATIBILITY NOTE FOR APP DEVELOPMENT — READ BEFORE WIRING TO PRICING LOGIC

The Carthage Transfer mobile app's existing pricing engine (night/weekend/last-minute/seasonal
surcharges, TND base fares of 80/120/180/150 for Standard/VIP/Luxury/Van, and a points-based loyalty
tier system) does NOT currently match what is published on the live website. The live site:
  - Prices in EUR, not TND
  - Uses flat "starting from" route pricing rather than a percentage-surcharge formula
  - Does not publish a loyalty/rewards programme
  - States cancellation penalties only vaguely ("may be subject to charges"), with no specific
    50%/100% tiers
This is a deliberate placeholder-vs-reality gap that needs an explicit decision: either (a) treat the
app's surcharge/loyalty system as an internal business rule layered on top of the base EUR pricing
shown here, and have AVA explain it as "your effective price, after applicable surcharges," or
(b) update the app's pricing engine to match the real published structure before going live. AVA's
RAG answers should not silently assume one or the other — when asked specifics about loyalty points
or surcharge percentages, the safer answer is to describe what the app currently calculates, while
being clear that public marketing pricing is shown in EUR and may differ from the app's quoted total.
