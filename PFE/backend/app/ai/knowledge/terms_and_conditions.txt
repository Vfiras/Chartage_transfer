CANCELLATION POLICY

Free cancellation with a full refund is available if you cancel at least 24 hours before your
scheduled pickup. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before pickup may be subject to charges.
(The published policy does not specify an exact cancellation fee percentage for late cancellations

WAITING TIME

One hour of free waiting time is included after your flight lands. Waiting time starts only after
landing, never before. Additional waiting beyond the free hour is charged at 5 EUR per 30 minutes.

---

FLIGHT TRACKING

Drivers track your flight using the flight number you provide, to ensure on-time pickup even in
case of delays.

PICKUP INSTRUCTIONS

Upon arrival, your driver will be waiting in the arrivals area, holding a sign with your name.
