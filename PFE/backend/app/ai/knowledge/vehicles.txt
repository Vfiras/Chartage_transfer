CHOOSING THE RIGHT VEHICLE

As a quick guide based on the real fleet:
- 1 passenger, light luggage → Toyota Yaris or Standard Sedan
- 2–4 passengers, professional context → Mercedes E-Class
- 2–3 passengers, premium/special occasion → Mercedes S-Class
- Family of up to 6–7 with luggage → Mercedes V-Class or VW Multivan
- 12+ passengers → Toyota Hiace, Toyota Coaster, Otokar, or MAN A51 depending on group size

COMPATIBILITY NOTE FOR APP DEVELOPMENT — READ BEFORE WIRING TO recommend_vehicle TOOL

The app's current `cars` collection and `recommend_vehicle` tool use a 4-category simplified model:
Standard, VIP, Luxury, Van. The real published fleet has 12 distinct vehicle models across those
broad categories (e.g. "VIP" in the app likely maps to Mercedes E-Class or S-Class on the real
fleet; "Van" likely maps to several different real vehicles from VW Multivan up to the MAN A51).
This is a deliberate simplification in the app, not an error — but if the app's seeded `cars`
documents are ever updated to match real fleet data, this mapping should be made explicit (e.g. a
`real_model` field) so AVA's vehicle descriptions stay consistent with both the app's internal
category and the actual vehicle a client would receive.
